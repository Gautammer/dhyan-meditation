Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Dhyan Meditation ("the app", "we", "us") is designed to work without an account and without sending your practice anywhere. This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your device, and your choices.

The short version

  • There is no sign-up and no user account.
  • Your settings, chosen background and meditation history are stored only on your device.
  • The only data that leaves your device relates to in-app purchases, handled by Apple or Google and our subscription provider, RevenueCat.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; no push service is used.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on your device:

  • Settings: candle options such as breeze, glow, embers, music, timer visibility, status bar and session length.
  • Background: the meditation scene you selected.
  • Meditation history: the start time, end time and length of each session, used for the Statistics screen.
  • Reminder: whether the daily reminder is on and the time you chose.

This information is never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes it.

In-app purchases

Dhyan Pro is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment is processed by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies; we never see your payment details.

To know whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-generated identifier, your purchase and subscription status, and basic device information (such as platform and app version). It does not receive your name, email address or meditation history. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

"Restore purchases" asks Apple or Google for purchases already made with your store account.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications and schedules a local notification on your device at the time you choose. Nothing is sent to a server. You can turn the reminder off in the app or withdraw permission in your device settings at any time.

Audio and screen

Ambient music plays from a file bundled with the app. The app does not use the microphone, camera, contacts, photos or location. While a session is running the app keeps the screen awake.

Sharing

The Share and Rate options open your device's share sheet or the app store page. Nothing is shared without your action.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your choices

  • Clear all app data by deleting the app.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings.
  • Disable reminders in the app or in your device's notification settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version is published on this page and the "Last updated" date above changes.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore Dhyan Pro on a new device?
Open the app, go to More → Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On iPhone or iPad open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Dhyan Meditation. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

How do I end a session early?
Double tap anywhere on the candle screen. Every session is still saved to your statistics.

The daily reminder doesn't appear.
Check that notifications are allowed for Dhyan in your device settings, then turn the reminder off and on again in the app's Settings tab.

Where is my data stored?
Only on your device. See our Privacy Policy.
